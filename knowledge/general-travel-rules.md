## Connection and layover risk
A layover shorter than 60 minutes on a domestic connection, or shorter than 90 minutes when changing to an international flight, is risky: a small delay can make you miss the next flight. Overnight layovers mean extra hotel cost.

## Self-transfer and separate tickets
If two flights are on separate tickets ("self-transfer"), the airline is not responsible if you miss the second flight because the first was late. You may also need to collect and re-check bags.

## Airport changes
Some cheap itineraries land at one airport and leave from another in the same city (for example arriving at LGA and departing from JFK). Allow several hours and ground transport cost.

## Basic economy in general
Basic economy fares usually mean no seat choice, boarding last, and no changes. On some airlines they also exclude a full-size carry-on bag, so the true cost can be higher than a regular fare.
