## Checked bag fees (US domestic, economy)
first_checked_bag_usd: 35
second_checked_bag_usd: 45
Southwest ended "bags fly free" for most fares in 2025. On Basic and Choice fares the first checked bag is about $35 and the second about $45. Some higher fares and elite members still get free bags.

## Carry-on
One carry-on bag and one personal item are included.

## Basic fare restrictions
Basic fare: seat assigned (assigned seating introduced from 2026), boards last, limited changes.
