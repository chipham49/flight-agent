## Checked bag fees (US domestic, economy)
first_checked_bag_usd: 40
second_checked_bag_usd: 50
First checked bag roughly $35-45 depending on travel date (peak dates cost more) when paid in advance; the second bag roughly $50-60. Value used here: typical midpoint.

## Carry-on
Blue and higher fares include one carry-on and one personal item.

## Blue Basic restrictions
Blue Basic (basic economy): personal item only on most routes; a full-size carry-on is not included. No seat selection, boards last, no changes.
