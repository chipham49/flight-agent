## Checked bag fees (US domestic, economy)
first_checked_bag_usd: 35
second_checked_bag_usd: 45
First checked bag about $35 and second about $45 for Main and Saver fares.

## Carry-on
One carry-on bag and one personal item included on all fares.

## Saver fare restrictions
Saver (basic economy): seat assigned at check-in, boards last, no changes or upgrades.
