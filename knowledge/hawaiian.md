## Checked bag fees (North America to Hawaii, economy)
first_checked_bag_usd: 40
second_checked_bag_usd: 45
Between North America and Hawaii, first bag about $40 and second about $45. Neighbor-island flights are cheaper. Hawaiian is merging with Alaska Airlines, so policies may change.

## Carry-on
One carry-on and one personal item included.

## Main Cabin Basic restrictions
Main Cabin Basic: no seat selection, boards last, no changes.
