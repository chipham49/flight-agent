## Checked bag fees (US domestic, economy)
first_checked_bag_usd: 35
second_checked_bag_usd: 45
First checked bag costs about $35 when prepaid online ($40 at the airport); second bag about $45 prepaid ($50 at the airport).

## Carry-on
Standard Economy includes one carry-on bag and one personal item.

## Basic Economy restrictions
Basic Economy on most domestic routes allows only one personal item that fits under the seat; a full-size carry-on bag is not included and must be checked for a fee plus a gate handling charge. No seat selection, boards last, no changes.
