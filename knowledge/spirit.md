Spirit prices bags dynamically by route and date. A first checked bag bought online is commonly about $45-65 and costs more at the airport. Value used here: typical online price. Always check the exact price for your flight.

## Carry-on
The base fare includes only a personal item. A carry-on bag costs extra (often similar to a checked bag). Bundles include bags.

## Base fare restrictions
Base fare: no seat selection, no carry-on, change fees may apply. This is the classic "cheap fare, expensive extras" case.
