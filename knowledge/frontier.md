## Checked bag fees (US domestic, economy)
first_checked_bag_usd: 60
second_checked_bag_usd: 75
Frontier prices bags dynamically. A checked bag bought during booking is commonly about $50-70 and much more at the airport. Value used here: typical booking price. Check the exact price for your flight.

## Carry-on
The basic fare includes only a personal item. A carry-on bag costs extra. Bundles (Economy, Premium, Business) include bags.

## Basic fare restrictions
Basic fare: no seat selection, no carry-on, change fees may apply.
