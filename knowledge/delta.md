## Checked bag fees (US domestic, economy)
first_checked_bag_usd: 35
second_checked_bag_usd: 45
Checked baggage fee for the first bag is about $35 and the second bag about $45 on domestic flights in Main Cabin and Basic Economy. Many international itineraries include one free checked bag.

## Carry-on
One carry-on bag and one personal item are included on all fares, including Basic Economy.

## Basic Economy restrictions
Basic Economy: no seat selection before check-in (seat assigned later), boards last, no changes allowed, no upgrades. Carry-on still included.
